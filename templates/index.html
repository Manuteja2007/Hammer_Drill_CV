BEFORE YOU BEGIN Hammer + golf ball

A clear view makes a better review.

  • Keep the ball and hammer head inside the frame.
  • Use steady lighting and a fixed camera position.
  • Leave space above the ball's highest point.
  • Pick the ball up to reset; hand contact should not count as a tap.
